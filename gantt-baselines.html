03 / Second supplied state · Manage Baselines

Give saved baselines a dedicated place.

The side panel lists Baseline 1, Baseline 2, and Baseline 3 with row-level action icons. The original screen keeps the Gantt canvas visible beside it.

Management and comparison have different surfaces: the menu chooses the active comparison; the panel presents the saved baselines. Screens behind Create New or the action icons, permission rules, and confirmation states are not supplied.

The panel crop preserves baseline names and controls. Author metadata rows have been redacted for privacy; they are not empty product states.

Design rationale / Reading the supplied interfaces

Separate the choice from the administration.

The design challenge is to compare a schedule without making every baseline-management action part of that reading task.

  1. 01

    Keep comparison selection close to the timeline.

    A compact submenu retains the schedule context. The trade-off is nested navigation and a small surface for explaining the selection rule.

  2. 02

    Make the comparison limit explicit.

    The two-option instruction sets a clear boundary before selection. The supplied state does not show how a third selection is prevented or explained.

  3. 03

    Keep baseline names consistent across surfaces.

    Baseline 1–3 appear in both the menu and manager. Matching names connect the saved references to the comparison choice; more descriptive names would be a future design question, not a tested improvement.
