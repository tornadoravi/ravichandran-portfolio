I design enterprise experiences that help people understand data, communicate insights, and manage complex planning workflows.

Over four years at Lumel Technologies, I’ve worked across PowerTable for Microsoft Fabric and Inforiver Analytics+ for Microsoft Power BI. My responsibilities span requirements, existing-experience reviews, user flows, prototypes, interaction and visual design, developer handoff, and implementation reviews.

My focus is on making powerful functionality understandable: a clear entry point, a coherent workflow, and the right level of detail at each step.

Currently Product Designer II at Lumel, since January 2025.

01 / Approach

From requirements
to implementation.

Designing an enterprise product means understanding both the system and the workflow it supports.

I work with product managers, developers, and stakeholders to connect requirements with interaction decisions and implementation detail. That includes reviewing the existing experience, mapping flows, developing prototypes, and checking the implemented product against the design.

01

Understand the workflow

Establish the requirements, existing controls, and sequence of decisions before designing individual screens.

02

Structure the interaction

Organize discovery, configuration, and deeper controls so the experience supports different levels of familiarity.

03

Carry the detail through

Stay involved in handoff and implementation reviews to maintain consistency between the design and the product.
